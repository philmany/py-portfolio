Oh, you’re here to learn about me.

Well, my work shares a lot with who I am.

I envision the bigger picture with awe - Microsoft and L’Oréal was about enterprise and scale.

I also revere going 0→1. Finding the shape of something before it has one. It’s building from human connection at each step.

At 16, I discovered the joy of building community and building FOR community. I built computers from recycled e-waste at Free Geek, a non-profit in East Vancouver. I saw my impact on families who couldn’t afford them, and I served their needs. Value shouldn’t be disposable. Make it flow somewhere, to someone.

That belief guides me today.

I find clarity on the water. Deer Lake (Burnaby). Lake Union (Seattle). Victoria Harbour (Hong Kong). Places that hold memory, and float you towards new ones.

What’s for you will always find you. You’ll bring yourself to it.

Say hello.
