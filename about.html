Hey there, I’m Philman.

I nurture ideas and people.

My work lives between systems and the humans inside them. At Microsoft, that meant AI and advertising safety for almost every country on the globe. At startups, it meant finding the shape of something before it had one.

I learned to build at sixteen, restoring discarded computers at FreeGeek in East Vancouver for families who couldn’t afford new ones. Nothing is ever truly thrown away. It flows somewhere, to someone.

That belief still guides me. Technology should be made with care, and measured by who it reaches and what it asks of them.

I find my clarity on the water. Deer Lake (Burnaby). Lake Union (Seattle). Victoria Harbour (Hong Kong). Places that hold memory, and give it back.

Good products work the same way. They channel what people need clearly, and leave the world gentler than they found it.
