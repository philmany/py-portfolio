01Problem

Background

Bandit Labs is an AI chat analytics platform that enables product teams with customer insights.

Find clarity for Agile product development through chat analytics.

Build ease between UX, CX and product.banditlabs.io

1280x800

1.1Problem

Product teams use customer feedback to influence their product roadmap and features.

However, the process of turning raw data into actionable insight is not straightforward.

🔨+ 💎+ ❓= 💍

Problem (2)

Example
A company with $2b online revenue collects data through web analytics and customer chat data.

With millions of customer interactions, how does a product team interface with CX teams and data for everyday work?

After the audit, the entire product was remapped to fit a user journey, to aid in Bandit Lab’s demo scenario to prospective customers.

3.2Product Solution

User Wins

For Product Managers and CX Teams, Bandit Labs surfaces data in a streamlined, easy to use way.

Rebecca can focus on product development, while the CX Director, Simon, no longer has the headache of sending weekly reports to the product team.

Business Wins

Bandit Labs needed productization to expedite their funding round and product-market-fit, so that they could scale. Massively.

This is the accessible, user-centric product to demo to a problem space that is hungry for their product.

Understanding the Customer’s Goals with Chat

This user flow displays the user experience where the customer’s user and customer service agents generate data that Bandit Labs can analyze. The purpose for documenting this step is to understand what goals chat accomplishes for the business. This is essential when setting up initial calls and demos in the sales process.

In the example below, VICHY uses LiveChat as a channel for:

  • Informing consumer inquiries with accurate product information, as a knowledge base for applying and using their products.
  • Answering requests regarding orders; dealing with more specific logistical issues related to their ecommerce system and orders.

This is evidence of defined categories in terms of conversation types. When the data types for the chat may remain the same, KPI goals of organizations differs
